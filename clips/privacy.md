# Lanes Clip Poster: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: {{ site.last_updated }}_

## What we access and store
- **Account sign-in tokens:** when you connect TikTok or YouTube, the platform gives the app access tokens. We store them encrypted and use them only to post the videos you approve and to read your basic account details (name, avatar, and the posting options the platform allows).
- **Your videos and captions:** stored only until they're posted, then deleted from the app within 30 days.
- **Post records:** the video ID, time posted and settings used, so you can see your posting history.

We don't read your followers, messages or other private account data. We don't sell any data or use it for advertising.

## Google and YouTube
The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements. Google's handling of your data is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can revoke the app's access at any time on your [Google security settings page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## TikTok
TikTok's handling of your data is covered by the [TikTok Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy). You can remove the app's access in TikTok under Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

## Deletion
If you disconnect an account or revoke access, we delete its tokens and post records within 30 days. To ask us to delete anything sooner, email {{ site.contact_email }}.
