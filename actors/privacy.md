# Apify Actors: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: {{ site.last_updated }}_

## What we see
Your Actor runs take place on Apify's platform, under your account. Your inputs and results are stored there, under [Apify's Privacy Policy](https://apify.com/privacy-policy). We don't copy your results to our own systems.

As the Actor's developer, Apify shows us:
- Usage numbers, such as run counts, success rates and costs, so we can keep the Actors working and priced fairly.
- Anything you send us yourself, such as an issue report, a shared run, or an email.

## What we do with it
We use it only to run, fix and improve the Actors and to answer your questions. We never sell it.

## The data the Actors collect
The Actors collect public data from websites. They are built not to collect private personal data such as private email addresses or phone numbers. If a page you point an Actor at contains personal data, you are responsible for handling the results lawfully.

## Deletion
Your runs, datasets and inputs live in your Apify account, and you can delete them there at any time. To have us delete anything you sent us directly, email {{ site.contact_email }}.
